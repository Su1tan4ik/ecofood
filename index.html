04 — Share

Got surplus?
Post it in 30 seconds.

The listing appears in the feed immediately. All data is stored locally — this demo sends nothing to a server.

A few rules, so everyone has a good time.

We don't control venues, but we stick to principles: safety, honesty and respect. If food is spoiled — don't post it. If you're running late — let them know.

  • 1.
    Check the expiry date. If less than 24 hours — add the "urgent" tag.
  • 2.
    Describe the state honestly: open/sealed, reheated or not.
  • 3.
    List allergens if you know them. Especially nuts, gluten, lactose.
  • 4.
    Be reachable during the listed hours — someone will come pick up.

By clicking "Publish" you agree to the community principles.
